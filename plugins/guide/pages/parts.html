5

Layout and the way through a site

Four parts set out a page. None of them has a width in pixels, so the page follows the screen.

  • A stack, ord-stack, puts parts under each other with one distance.
  • A row, ord-row, puts parts beside each other, and wraps them on a narrow screen.
  • A grid, ord-grid, makes as many columns of equal width as the screen has room for.
  • A split, ord-split, puts a narrow column beside a wide one. On a narrow screen they stand under each other.
