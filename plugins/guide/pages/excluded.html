Excluded

Ordinance DS leaves some things out on purpose. This page lists them, and gives the reason for each.

1

Left out on purpose

Each of these would bring security work of its own. A part that is half built would be the weakest part of the framework.

2

Left to you

Ordinance DS covers what is sent to the browser. It does not cover how your team works, and it does not say which laws apply to your product.

You can use any engineering rules with Ordinance DS. The only condition is that they keep the rules on the page Checks.

3

The rule for this list

When something is left out of Ordinance DS, it is left out everywhere. It has no page, no item in the menu and no name in the stylesheet.
