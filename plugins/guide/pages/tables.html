Tables

A regulated product is mostly tables. The table component has variants for its room, its lines, its columns and its length.

1

Room

Three sizes change the room around the text. The text itself keeps its size, so a compact table is still easy to read.

2

Stripes and lines

Stripes help the eye to follow a wide row. Lines between the columns help when the cells hold numbers or codes.

Variants can be used together. The second table is compact and has lines.

3

Columns

A class on a column says how its text breaks and how wide it is.

  • ord-table__narrow makes a column as narrow as its text.
  • ord-table__wide gives a column about half of the table.
  • ord-table__nowrap keeps a date or a name on one line.
  • ord-table__break breaks a long identifier, so that it does not push the table wider.
  • ord-table__num puts a number at the end of its cell, with digits of equal width.

The variant fixed gives all columns the same width. The table then never depends on the length of its text.

4

A long table

A long table scrolls inside its frame, and its head stays in view. The last row holds a sum.

The frame takes the focus of the keyboard, so the arrow keys scroll it. A wide table scrolls sideways in the same frame on a narrow screen.
