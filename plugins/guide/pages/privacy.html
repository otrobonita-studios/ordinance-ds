Privacy

A page that asks no other server tells no other server about its reader. Ordinance DS keeps to that.

1

What the pages do

  • The pages load their styles, scripts and typefaces from the site itself.
  • The pages contain no analytics.
  • Ordinance DS sets no cookie.
  • A script of a plugin may not store anything in the browser. The check refuses localStorage and similar stores.
  • A link to another site does not say which page the reader came from.
2

The rule that this answers

The GDPR asks for data protection by design and by default, in Article 25. Loading nothing from others is one way to design for it.

Your product still decides which personal data it shows. Ordinance DS cannot check that.
