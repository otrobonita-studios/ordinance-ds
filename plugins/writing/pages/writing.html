Writing

Many readers of a regulated product do not have English as their first language. Ordinance DS checks its own pages against plain-language rules.

1

The rules

Text inside code is not checked.

2

Words to avoid

3

Use your own rules

The rules are two files in the folder of this plugin. Change the numbers and the words to follow the specification that you use.

ISO 24495-1 is an international standard for plain language. Ordinance DS names it, and does not copy its text.

4

What the check cannot judge

A script counts words and finds phrases. It cannot judge whether a text is true, clear or kind.
